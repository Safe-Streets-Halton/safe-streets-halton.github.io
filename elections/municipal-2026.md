# Election Day: <time datetime="2026-10-26" title="2026-10-26">Monday, October 26, 2026</time>

Have a say in how your city runs!
Whether you care about housing, transit, schools, or community services,
voting this October is the most impactful way to change the way your neighbourhood grows to fit your needs.

The next Ontario municipal elections are this Fall.
Vote for your Mayor, Regional Councillor, Local Councillor, and School Board Trustee.
Find candidate lists, voting locations, and advance voting dates on your city's official election page below.

* [City of Burlington — Elections](https://www.burlington.ca/en/council-and-city-administration/elections.aspx)
* [Town of Oakville — 2026 Municipal Election](https://www.oakville.ca/town-hall/elections/)
* [Town of Milton — 2026 Municipal Election](https://www.milton.ca/en/town-hall/2026-municipal-election.aspx)
* [Town of Halton Hills — Municipal Elections](https://www.haltonhills.ca/election)

Be sure to register to vote with [Elections Ontario](https://www.registertovoteon.ca) before August 12.

Not sure which city you're in? Check the [Halton Region elections page](https://www.halton.ca/the-region/regional-council-and-committees/municipal-elections) for an overview.
